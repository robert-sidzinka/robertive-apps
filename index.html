Axe Thrower

SHARPEN · THROW · REIGN

A skill-based axe-throwing game for Android. Sharpen your blade, master the throw, and climb from a rookie tournament thrower to a champion.

Career Campaign

A full RPG career — stats, perks, an economy with sponsors and side bets, and a ladder of rival throwers to beat.

Quick Game

Jump straight in across three difficulties, or take on the one-seed-a-day Daily Gauntlet.

Leaderboards

Compare your daily score against the world and against your own crew of friends.

Challenges & Versus

Star-rated trick challenges, plus local pass-and-play versus for head-to-head matches.
