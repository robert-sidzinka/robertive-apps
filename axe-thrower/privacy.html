Privacy Policy

Axe Thrower · Last updated: 28 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Robertive Robert Sidzinka ("we", "us") collects and uses information in the mobile game Axe Thrower (the "App"). We are based in Poland and process personal data in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

The App does not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

1. Information we collect

Analytics data. We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the App is used — for example which screens are opened, how long sessions last, and which game modes are played. This includes a pseudonymous app-instance identifier and basic device information (device model, operating system version, language, and country).

Crash and diagnostic data. We use Google Firebase Crashlytics to detect and fix crashes. When the App crashes we receive a crash report containing the error, a stack trace, and the device state at the time.

Anonymous identifier. To power online features (leaderboards and the friend-referral system) the App signs in with Firebase Anonymous Authentication. This creates a random, anonymous user ID that is not linked to your real identity.

Data you choose to provide. If you take part in the online leaderboard or referral features, we store the display name you enter, your daily-gauntlet score, your friend code, and referral relationships, associated with your anonymous ID, in Google Cloud Firestore. Please do not enter personal information as your display name, as it may be visible to other players.

2. How we use this information

We use the information above to operate and improve the App, to run leaderboards and referrals, to fix crashes and bugs, and to understand overall usage trends. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in operating and improving the App and, where required, your consent.

3. Advertising

The App does not show third-party ads. Analytics signals are explicitly not used for ad personalization.

4. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell your data. Data is processed on our behalf by Google (Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Authentication, and Cloud Firestore) as our service provider, and by Google Play for app distribution. Google's handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. Our Cloud Firestore data is stored in Google's European (EU) data centres.

5. Data retention

Analytics and crash data are retained according to Google Firebase's default retention periods. Leaderboard entries are tied to a daily seed and remain on that day's board. You may ask us to delete data associated with your anonymous ID at any time (see "Your rights").

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at robertive.apps@gmail.com. Because online features use an anonymous ID, please include your friend code so we can locate the relevant records.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

8. Security

We rely on Google's infrastructure security to protect the data described above. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to safeguard your information.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
